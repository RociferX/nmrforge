# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

This project is a scientific software project. Its contributors are expected to be able to
disagree about code, methods and data without being treated badly for it. We are committed to
making participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This document is written specifically for this project rather than copied from a template. If the
maintainers later prefer a standard text, the
[Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
should be adopted verbatim, with an enforcement contact filled in.

## Expected behaviour

- Be precise and technical in disagreement. "This parameter does not do what you think on this
  NMRPipe version, here is the evidence" is welcome; "this is obviously wrong" is not.
- Accept that a wrong scientific result is a legitimate reason to reject a change, and that the
  burden of evidence falls on the person proposing a change to processing behaviour.
- Respect data ownership. Never post someone else's unpublished data, sample names, laboratory
  paths, spectra or results - in an issue, a pull request, or a screenshot.
- Assume that a maintainer who asks "is this verifiable?" is protecting the users of the results,
  not questioning your competence.
- Give credit accurately. Do not add an author to citation metadata without their agreement, and
  do not remove one who contributed.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, personal attacks, insults, or discriminatory comments.
- Sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Publishing private information - including the data and unpublished results described above -
  without explicit permission.
- Deliberately misrepresenting results, measurements or provenance.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or dismissing reports of unacceptable behaviour.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, commit messages, code comments,
documentation, and any public forum where someone is representing the project.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obliged to

clarification, through a warning, to a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces, decided by
the maintainers and recorded.
